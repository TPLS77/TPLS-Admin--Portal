📘 Teacher Rules & Expectations

  • 1
    Professional conduct
    Treat students, parents and colleagues with respect, patience and professionalism.
  • 2
    Punctuality & attendance
    Arrive on time, follow the school timetable and inform administration about unavoidable absence.
  • 3
    Student safeguarding
    Protect student dignity, privacy and safety. Report serious concerns to administration promptly.
  • 4
    Academic responsibility
    Prepare lessons, maintain appropriate records and support students according to their needs.
  • 5
    Confidentiality
    Do not share student, family, financial or school information outside authorized channels.
  • 6
    Portal responsibility
    Keep your password private and sign out when using a shared computer.
These are the portal's initial general rules. Your school administration can later replace them with the official TPLS staff handbook/rules.
