Designation Duties
TeacherTeaching, lesson plans, attendance, assessment, results, student progress and reports.
Financial SecretaryFees, receipts, expenses, outstanding fees, salary/payment records and financial reports.
AccountantFinancial records, verification, reconciliation, statements, balances and audit support.
High Section CoordinatorHigh-section coordination, lesson progress, schedules, attendance/results and reporting to Admin.
Low Section CoordinatorLow-section coordination, lesson progress, schedules, attendance/results and reporting to Admin.
PrincipalSchool leadership, staff/academic oversight, welfare, administration, finances, reports, approvals and communication.
AdminStudents, teachers, classes, fees, expenses, reports, requests, notifications and school records.
Super AdminFull school system, users, settings, records, financial and administrative control.
Teaching Staff Access
Every designated staff member may teach. Their designation does not remove teaching features; add their teaching assignments below whenever applicable.

Teaching Assignments

Teaching access is additive: every staff designation can also be a teaching staff member. Select each class and subject this person teaches, whether their designation is Teacher, Financial Secretary, Accountant, Coordinator, Administrator, or another role.
